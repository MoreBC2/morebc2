# Exchange Listing Target Matrix

**Category:** Documentation
**Status:** Draft
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-02

## Summary

This page tracks exchanges and service providers that may be relevant to native blockchain coin listings.

The goal is not to recommend every exchange. The goal is to separate:

- Officially documented listing paths.
- Publicly documented fees.
- Native-coin compatibility signals.
- Open questions that still need direct checking.

BC2 is a native coin, not a token. For that reason, native-asset support is more important than generic token-listing volume.

## Legend

- **Native signal:** Public information suggests support for independent coins or mainnet integrations.
- **Token-focused:** Public page mostly describes tokens or token applications.
- **Public fee:** Fee shown directly by the exchange.
- **Negotiated/unknown:** No fixed fee found in the official source checked.
- **Needs contact:** Application or helpdesk contact is required before details are known.

## Risk notes

### TradeOgre

Do not add TradeOgre as an active target without fresh legal and operational review. There have been public reports of enforcement or seizure activity. This repository should avoid recommending it until current status is directly verified from reliable sources.

### Token-only exchanges

Some exchanges advertise large numbers of listings but mostly support contract tokens. They may not be useful for BC2 unless they explicitly support native chain wallet integrations.

### Third-party listing agencies

Third-party pages often publish estimated listing fees. These may be useful for context, but they are not official exchange terms. Do not record them as confirmed fees unless the exchange itself publishes the fee.
