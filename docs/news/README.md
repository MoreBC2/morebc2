# News

**Category:** News  
**Status:** Reviewed framework  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## Summary

News is for **dated, factual BitcoinII (BC2) ecosystem updates** that can be tied to a durable source or a clearly described MoreBC2 direct check.

The section currently contains only this index. Existing releases, service observations, and verification events are documented in their technical/evidence sections rather than being duplicated here as pseudo-news posts.

## What belongs here

Good candidates include:

- a newly published BitcoinII Core release;
- a confirmed wallet/explorer/pool/exchange launch or retirement;
- a material repository or documentation milestone;
- a confirmed infrastructure status change;
- an upstream announcement with a durable source;
- a MoreBC2 verification milestone where the date and evidence record are explicit.

## Evidence requirements

Every news item should state:

- **what happened**;
- **when it happened**;
- **who/what the source is**;
- whether the item is an upstream announcement, public-service observation, or MoreBC2 verification result;
- what remains unverified, if anything.

For changing services, record a direct-check date. A service being active at one point in time does not prove permanent availability.

## Current-versus-historical routing

News is chronological reporting, not the canonical home for long-lived technical facts.

After an event:

- current technical behavior belongs in Documentation / Architecture / Source Atlas;
- current service status belongs in Ecosystem / Infrastructure;
- detailed tests belong in Verification;
- past milestones belong in History;
- interpretation/opinion belongs in Discussion.

A news page may link to those canonical records rather than restating them in full.

## Rules

- Date every news item.
- Cite the primary or strongest available source.
- Do not publish rumors as factual news.
- Avoid price hype, promotional language, and market predictions.
- Do not call a third-party service official unless an authoritative source establishes that status.
- Do not treat a listing announcement as proof of deposits, withdrawals, uptime, or compatibility.
- Do not treat a release announcement as binary-authentication or reproducible-build proof.
- If later evidence changes the status, link the newer record or add an explicit correction/update note.

## 2026-09-12 audit result
