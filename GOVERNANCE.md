# MoreBC2 Governance

**Status:** Framework

## Current model

MoreBC2 starts as a community documentation project.

The project is maintained by its first steward, with room for future contributors, reviewers, editors, and technical reviewers.

## Stewardship

A steward is responsible for:

- Keeping information organized.
- Encouraging source-backed contributions.
- Separating documentation from opinion.
- Welcoming corrections.
- Avoiding unsupported claims.
- Helping contributors find the right place for their work.

A steward does not define BitcoinII. Official BitcoinII sources remain authoritative.

## Contributor roles

### Contributor

Anyone who opens an issue, suggests a correction, submits documentation, or improves wording.

### Reviewer

Someone who checks clarity, formatting, links, and category placement.

### Technical reviewer

Someone who checks technical claims against source code, releases, or live network behavior.

### Maintainer

Someone trusted to merge changes and help guide project standards.

## Decision process

For small documentation fixes, maintainers may merge when the change is clearly correct.

For technical claims, the change should include sources.

For controversial topics, proposals, speculation, or community opinions, content should be placed in Discussion rather than Documentation.

## Disagreements

When there is disagreement:

1. Identify the source being used.
2. Prefer primary sources over secondary sources.
3. Clearly label uncertainty.
4. Move unresolved claims to Needs Review.
5. Avoid presenting disputed claims as verified documentation.

## Relationship to BitcoinII

MoreBC2 is community-maintained.

It should complement official BitcoinII resources, not replace them.

When MoreBC2 and official BitcoinII sources disagree, official BitcoinII sources take precedence until the difference is understood and documented.
