# MoreBC2 Founding Principles

## Mission

MoreBC2 exists to preserve, organize, and expand publicly verifiable knowledge about the BitcoinII (BC2) ecosystem.

## Philosophy

MoreBC2 is not the source of truth.

MoreBC2 is a guide to the source of truth.

When MoreBC2 and an official BitcoinII source disagree, the official source takes precedence. MoreBC2 should update, clarify, or correct its own content rather than trying to redefine the project.

## Values

- Accuracy over speed.
- Sources over speculation.
- Documentation over opinion.
- Community over ownership.
- Transparency over authority.
- Corrections over ego.

## Editorial rules

- Every technical claim should point back to a primary source when possible.
- Research is not documentation.
- Discussion is not implementation.
- Opinion belongs in clearly labeled opinion or discussion spaces.
- Historical information should be labeled as historical.
- Uncertainty should be admitted clearly.
- Pages should never imply that a feature exists unless it can be verified in current releases, source code, or official project materials.

## Content categories

### Documentation

Current, verifiable information about how BitcoinII works today.

### News

Factual updates about things that happened.

### Research

Technical topics, comparisons, and analysis that may be useful to the ecosystem but are not necessarily part of BitcoinII.

### Discussion

Community ideas, proposals, opinions, and open questions.

### History

Past events, milestones, older documentation, and archived context.

## Page standard

Every important page should answer:

1. What is it?
2. Why does it matter?
3. How does it work?
4. Where can it be verified?

## Success

MoreBC2 succeeds when someone says:

> I don't know. Let's verify it.

instead of:

> Trust me.
